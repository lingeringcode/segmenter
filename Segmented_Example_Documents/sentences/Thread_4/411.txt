NOTE:
Thyroid RFA is FDA cleared, not FDA approved.
FDA approval differs from FDA clearance.
FDA approval means a treatment has been rigorously tested through clinical trials and is proven safe and effective.
In contrast, clearance is reserved for a treatment that is equivalent to an existing FDA-approved treatment.
