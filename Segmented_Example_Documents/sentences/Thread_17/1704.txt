First-
regardless of the date of service- check the date it was processed.
If the provider only recently submitted these claims- you may have more time.
Second-
even if you can't do an internal appeal, most insurances have an external appeal process.
Third-
most insurances have a 6 month timely filing limit, so as long as they submit that first bill to the insurance that was active at the time- then even if it denies, there will be proof it filed in time.
Then they can re- examine it, and at worst- partially process it from 23rd of Dec onward.
