Deep cleanings generally aren't covered except under very certain circumstances.
They must be diagnosed as medically necessary, and many plans require authorization for them.
Deep cleanings aren't routine dental services, and the coverage restrictions exist because the process of root planing and scaling is considered invasive and can include the risk of infection.
You need to find out under what circumstances your insurance covers these cleanings.
If you don't meet the required guidelines for the procedure, they won't pay.
