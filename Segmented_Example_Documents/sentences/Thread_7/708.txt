These aren’t issues for you to worry about.
You aren’t responsible for determining medical necessity.
In NICUs, there are different levels.
If a doctor kept your child at one level longer than necessary, that’s the them/the hospital.
You won’t receive a bill because of it.
Submitting additional medical info is totally normal and also the hospital’s responsibility.
Sometimes there is a lot of back and forth with hospitals and networks before a claim is paid.
This is all normal and I wouldn’t be concerned until you receive an actual bill from the hospital
