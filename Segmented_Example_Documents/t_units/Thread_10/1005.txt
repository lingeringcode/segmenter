You can certainly appeal it but
honestly I would n’t expect them to overturn the out of network processing unless there was a legitimate network adequacy issue
The only real argument would be if there were literally no in network imaging providers available within the geographic access standard required by law
If there were in network imaging centers available within that required distance the fact that you had the imaging done at an out of network facility generally is n't going to be enough to get the claim reprocessed at the in network benefit level
I ca n’t imagine there being absolutely no in network imaging options within the applicable radius
At the very least there is likely an in network hospital offering outpatient imaging
So yes you can appeal but
unless you can demonstrate that you did n't have a reasonably accessible in network option I would n't expect the appeal to be successful
I just do n't want to give you false hope that the insurance company is likely to simply make an exception after the fact
